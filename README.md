# Pure-Stream

# 🎓 PureStream — Ad-Free Student Study & Music Hub (Long Videos & Direct Search)

> **Students ke liye bina kisi ads ke lambe lectures (One-Shots, Marathons) aur gaane (Non-Stop Jukebox) dekhne aur sunane ke liye banayi gayi website.**
> **Ab YouTube jakar bar-bar link copy karne ki zaroorat nahi hai — seedhe website par hi search karein!**

---

## 🌟 Students & Music Lovers ke Liye Naye Features

### 1. 🔍 Direct YouTube Search (No Link Copy Needed)
- Website ke upar diye gaye search bar me kisi bhi topic, chapter ya gaane ka naam likhein:
  - Jaise: `Physics Wallah Class 12 One Shot`, `Arijit Singh 3 Hr Jukebox`, `Python Full Course 10 Hours`, `Khan Sir GS Marathon`.
  - Type karte hi **Live YouTube Suggestions** dikhenge.
  - Enter dabate hi saare videos **Search Results** tab me live load ho jayenge.
  - Bas 1-click me koi bhi video bina ads ke chalega!

### 2. ⏳ Long Videos Only Filter (Lambe Lectures & Marathons)
- Search bar me **"Long Videos Only"** switch diya gaya hai.
- Isse faltu ke chhote clips aur shorts filter ho jate hain aur sirf **20 minute se lekar 12 ghante tak ke complete lectures, one-shot revision marathons aur full song albums** samne aate hain.

### 3. ⏱️ Smart Resume (Jahan Chhoda Tha, Wahi Se Shuru)
- 4 se 8 ghante ke lambe lectures ko ek bar me dekhna mushkil hota hai.
- PureStream aapke lecture ka exact second browser me secretly yaad rakhta hai.
- Jab aap agle din ya baad me lecture dubara kholenge, to ye prompt dega:
  > *"⏱️ Aapne is lecture ko 02:45:10 par chhod diya tha. [Resume Wahi Se ▶]"*

### 4. 📝 Lecture Timestamp Notes (Notes & Bookmarks)
- Lecture dekhte waqt jaise hi koi important formula, concept ya derivation aaye:
  - **"+ Add Note"** button dabayein (ya keyboard par `N` dabayein).
  - Note likhein (jaise: `Gauss Law Derivation start`).
  - Video ke niche clickable timestamp badge ban jayega (`[01:15:20]`).
  - Kisi bhi waqt us badge par click karke aap direct video ke us part par jump kar sakte hain!
  - Padhai khatam hone par **"Export Notes (.txt)"** se saare notes ek text file me download karein.

### 5. ⏱️ Pomodoro Study Timer (25m / 50m)
- Concentration badhane ke liye built-in Pomodoro timer:
  - 25 Mins Focus + 5 Mins Break (Standard)
  - 50 Mins Deep Study + 10 Mins Break (Marathon)
  - Time khatam hone par sweet audio chime chime alert bajega!

### 6. 🎧 Distraction-Free Audio / Study Mode
- Screen ki visuals ko band/dim karke sirf audio sunne ke liye **"Focus Mode"** toggle.
- Padhai me aankhon par strain kam karta hai aur battery/data bachata hai.

### 7. ⚡ Student Speed Controls
- Revision fast karne ke liye: **1x, 1.25x, 1.5x, 1.75x, 2x**.
